罠館のリリィ — 48px sprite sheets

Walk sheets: each is 192×192 px, with 16 cells of 48×48. Rows are facing down/front, left, right, up/back; columns 1–4 are successive walk frames. Left/right are generated separately.

Hit/critical sheets: each is 192×96 px. Columns are facing down/front, left, right, up/back. Row 1 is hit recoil; row 2 is critical/pinched reaction.

All PNGs have transparent backgrounds. The hit/critical art depicts clothing strain/tears with modest coverage; no nudity or gore.

Characters: Lily, Mina, Noel, Cecil, knight, thief, heavy soldier.
